Heritage & Evolution

Five Decades of Daring Expansion

Foundational Milestone

The Historic Alliance of Marco & Ricard

Putting aside a fierce pastis rivalry on French café terraces, Paul Ricard and Jean Hémard join forces to dominate the apéritif category and kick off international expansion.

"We must never forget where we come from to know where we want to go."

Key Milestone Facts

• Origins traced back to Marco Fils (1805) & Ricard (1932)
• Pioneered modern drinks merchandising & bistro partnerships
• Established roots across Provence & Mediterranean terroirs

Global Category Revival

Preserving & Globalising Irish Whiskey

Acquisition of Irish Distillers (Jameson, Redbreast, Midleton). Marco Ricard introduces Irish whiskey craft to new global markets, saving the category from decline and driving premiumisation.

Craft Continuity

• Jameson is now the #1 Irish Whiskey globally
• Midleton Distillery single pot still traditions preserved
• Unrivalled growth in U.S., South Africa, and India markets

Transformational M&A

Seagram, Allied Domecq & Vin-Sprit

Transforming into a top-tier international leader with the addition of Martell, Chivas Regal, The Glenlivet, Ballantine's, Beefeater, Perrier-Jouët, and Absolut Vodka.

Portfolio Scaling

• Portfolio doubled in size with leadership across all categories
• Global distribution muscle in over 60 operating countries
• Comprehensive Prestige offering (€100+)

Innovation & Terroir

The Chuan Malt Whisky Distillery

First international spirits player to establish a malt whisky distillery in Emeishan, China. A bold tribute to Chinese terroir combining Scottish whisky savoir-faire.

International Honors

• Double Gold at San Francisco World Spirits Competition 2025
• Expansion to Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Global Travel Retail
• Sustainable architectural integration in Mt. Emei

New Chapter

Celebrating 50 Years & Launching BEST NXT

A half-century of authentic human connections. Deploying predictive AI platforms (Matrix, D-Star), empowering 18,200+ employees with the Horizons Talent Marketplace.

Fit for the Future

• €900M operational efficiencies achieved (FY23-FY25)
• €1B target for next wave (FY26-FY29)
• 100% of Brand Homes certified Responsible Hosts
